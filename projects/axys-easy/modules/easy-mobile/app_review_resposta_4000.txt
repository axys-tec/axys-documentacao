2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE

AxysEasy Mobile is a free reference tool for construction cost estimation in Brazil, for engineers, architects, cost estimators, procurement officers, contract auditors and students.

The problem: Brazilian construction cost references are published as large spreadsheets and PDFs, updated monthly, and are impractical to consult on site. The app makes 12,234 supply items and 17,942 cost compositions searchable in seconds, with prices for all 27 Brazilian states and price history since November 2021.

Free. No advertising, no in-app purchases, no paid content.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS

An account is required because the app provides per-user features: price history charts, cost simulation with user-defined parameters, and PDF report generation. Registration happens in the app with e-mail verification. Demo credentials are in App Review Information.

After signing in, use the bottom navigation: Central de Custos (search and consult costs - the core feature), Nosso Conteudo (technical articles and glossary), Mais (account, contact, institutional).

No sample files needed. Search "alvenaria" or "concreto" in Central de Custos, then tap a result to see the cost breakdown. Account deletion: Mais > Minha Conta > Excluir minha conta.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

The app communicates only with infrastructure operated by Axys Engenharia e Tecnologia Ltda:
- easy-mobile.axys-tec.com.br (our REST API, read-only catalogue)
- public.axys-tec.com.br (our static content)
- www.axys-tec.com.br (our website: privacy policy, terms, support)

Authentication is our own identity service, not a third party. No payment processors, no advertising networks, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no AI services called at runtime. Third-party dependencies are standard open-source Flutter packages for HTTP, secure storage, PDF rendering and opening URLs.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app functions identically in all regions. Content is Brazilian Portuguese only and the cost data is Brazilian by nature. Distributed in Brazil only. No region-gated features, no regional content variations, no regional pricing - the app is free.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND AUTHORISATION

The app does not redistribute third-party cost tables. It presents Axys's own processed cost data - calculated, normalised and maintained by our engineering team - derived from reference systems used in Brazilian public procurement, as every construction cost estimation system in this market does.

The reference systems are SINAPI (maintained by Caixa Economica Federal and IBGE; Federal Decree 7.983/2013 establishes it as the mandatory cost reference for federally funded public works and requires its publication; freely available at www.caixa.gov.br), CDHU (Sao Paulo state housing company) and FDE (Sao Paulo state education foundation).

Axys maintains lawful access to these reference systems and performs its own processing, unit normalisation, historical series construction and cost calculation. The app states, in its App Store description and inside the app, that Axys is not the issuing body of these references and does not replace their official publications.

The app does not provide regulated professional, financial or medical services.
