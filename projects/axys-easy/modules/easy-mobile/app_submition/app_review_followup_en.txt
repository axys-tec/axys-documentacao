ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS

Demo credentials are in App Review Information (Sign-In Information). They are
current and verified working as of today.

After signing in, the app opens on the home screen. The bottom navigation bar
gives access to everything:

- Central de Custos - the core feature. Search "alvenaria" or "concreto", then
  tap any result to see the full cost breakdown of that item, its price history
  chart, and the technical description document.
- Nosso Conteudo - technical articles, newsletters and glossary.
- Mais - account settings, contact information, and institutional pages.

Account deletion: Mais > Minha Conta > Excluir minha conta. The app asks for
the password, confirms, and returns to the entry screen once complete. The
deleted account can no longer sign in.

No sample files are required and no feature is behind payment.

ADDITIONAL QUALITY ASSURANCE

Since our reply we completed another round of testing on a physical device
(iPhone 16 Pro Max, iOS 26.6), covering the full flow end to end: registration
with e-mail verification, sign-in, catalogue search and cost consultation,
technical documents, editorial content, in-app account deletion, and a failed
login attempt confirming the deleted account no longer authenticates.

That round found and fixed two issues, both included in the submitted build
1.0.0 (4):

- Account deletion could fail without informing the user. Failures are now
  shown clearly, and the app returns to the entry screen once deletion
  completes.
- The bottom navigation bar was displayed on screens where it could not
  operate (sign-in, registration, verification). It is now hidden there.
