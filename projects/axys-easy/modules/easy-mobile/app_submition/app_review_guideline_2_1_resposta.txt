2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE

AxysEasy Mobile is a free reference tool for construction cost estimation in
Brazil. It gives engineers, architects, cost estimators, public procurement
officers, contract auditors and students on-site access to construction cost
references used in Brazilian public works.

The problem it solves: these cost references are published as large
spreadsheets and PDF documents, updated monthly. Professionals working on site,
in meetings or in the field have no practical way to consult them. The app
makes 12,234 supply items and 17,942 cost compositions searchable in seconds,
with prices for all 27 Brazilian states and price history since November 2021.

The app is free. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no paid
content.


3. SETUP AND ACCESS

An account is required because the app provides per-user features: price
history charts, cost simulation with user-defined parameters (labour charges
and BDI), and generation of PDF analysis documents.

Registration is done inside the app (full name, e-mail, phone number) with
verification by e-mail or WhatsApp. Demo credentials are provided in the App
Review Information section.

After signing in, the main features are reached from the bottom navigation bar:

  Central de Custos  - search and consult cost items (the core feature)
  Nosso Conteudo     - technical articles, newsletters and glossary
  Mais               - account, contact and institutional information

No sample files are required. To reach the core feature immediately, open
Central de Custos and search for "alvenaria" or "concreto", then tap any
result to see the detailed cost breakdown.

Account deletion is available at Mais -> Minha Conta -> Excluir minha conta.


4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

The app communicates exclusively with infrastructure operated by Axys
Engenharia e Tecnologia Ltda:

  easy-mobile.axys-tec.com.br  - our own REST API (read-only catalogue access)
  public.axys-tec.com.br       - our own static content delivery
  www.axys-tec.com.br          - our own website (privacy policy, terms,
                                 support pages)

Authentication is handled by our own identity service, not by a third party.

There are no payment processors, no advertising networks, no third-party
analytics SDKs, and no AI services called by the app or by its API at runtime.

The app's only third-party dependencies are standard open-source Flutter
packages for HTTP requests, secure credential storage, PDF rendering and
opening external URLs.

Data sources are described in item 6.


5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

The app functions identically in all regions. Content is in Brazilian
Portuguese only and the cost data is Brazilian by nature. The app is
distributed in Brazil only. There are no region-gated features, no regional
content variations and no regional pricing - the app is free everywhere it is
available.


6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL AND AUTHORISATION

The app does not redistribute third-party cost tables. It presents Axys's own
processed cost data - calculated, normalised and maintained by our engineering
team - derived from reference systems used in Brazilian public procurement, in
the same way every construction cost estimation system in this market operates.

The reference systems are:

  SINAPI - Sistema Nacional de Pesquisas de Custos e Indices da Construcao
           Civil, maintained by Caixa Economica Federal and IBGE. Federal
           Decree 7.983/2013 establishes SINAPI as the mandatory cost reference
           for federally funded public works and requires its publication. It
           is freely published at www.caixa.gov.br.

  CDHU   - Companhia de Desenvolvimento Habitacional e Urbano do Estado de Sao
           Paulo, a Sao Paulo state company.

  FDE    - Fundacao para o Desenvolvimento da Educacao, Sao Paulo state.

Axys Engenharia e Tecnologia Ltda maintains lawful access to these reference
systems and performs its own processing, unit normalisation, historical series
construction and cost calculation over them.

The app states, in its App Store description and inside the app, that Axys is
not the issuing body of these references and that the app does not replace
their official publications.

The app does not provide regulated professional, financial or medical services.

ROTEIRO DA GRAVACAO (item 1) - fazer em iPhone fisico, sem cortes
================================================================================

1.  Comecar na tela inicial do iPhone e tocar no icone do app.
    (A Apple pede explicitamente que comece pelo LANCAMENTO. Video que comeca
    com o app ja aberto costuma voltar com o mesmo pedido.)

2.  Tela de login. Tocar em criar conta.

3.  Preencher o cadastro com um e-mail NOVO, nunca usado.
    Atencao: e-mail ja cadastrado devolve HTTP 409 e o app mostra
    "Requisicao recusada pelo servico de contas", que nao explica a causa.

4.  Mostrar a verificacao (codigo por e-mail) e concluir o cadastro.

5.  Central de Custos: buscar "alvenaria".

6.  Abrir uma composicao e mostrar o detalhamento (composicao analitica).

7.  Abrir o Caderno Tecnico da composicao.

8.  Voltar e abrir Nosso Conteudo, depois um artigo.

9.  Ir em Mais -> Minha Conta.

10. Tocar em "Excluir minha conta" e CONCLUIR a exclusao.
    Este e o trecho que a Apple mais quer ver: exclusao de conta e obrigatoria
    em apps com cadastro (Guideline 5.1.1(v)).

Gravar com a tela inteira visivel, sem pressa entre os toques.
